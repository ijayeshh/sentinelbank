# Escalation Matrix

**Policy OPS-900 · Retail Banking Operations Manual · Effective January 1, 2025**

## 900.1 Purpose

This matrix defines which situations a Relationship Manager must escalate, to whom, and within what timeframe. Escalation is mandatory, not discretionary. An RM who is uncertain whether a situation qualifies **must escalate**; there is no penalty for an unnecessary escalation and no exception for customer urgency or customer objection.

## 900.2 Escalation to Compliance (BSA/AML) — Highest Priority

Escalate to the **BSA/AML Compliance Officer immediately, before taking any further action on the request**:

- **Any OFAC or sanctions list hit** on a customer, signer, beneficial owner, wire beneficiary, originator, or intermediary bank — including partial and "possible" name matches. **This is a hard stop.** The RM must not release the transaction, must not close or restructure the account, must not notify the customer of the match, and must not clear the alert as a false positive under any circumstance. Only Compliance may adjudicate a sanctions hit. The transaction remains blocked until Compliance issues a written disposition.
- Structuring indicators, including cash activity deliberately kept under **$10,000**.
- Any request to alter, backdate, or omit a Currency Transaction Report.
- Suspected human trafficking, elder financial exploitation, or narcotics proceeds.
- Subpoenas, levies, or law enforcement inquiries of any kind.

Compliance escalations are logged the same business day. A Suspicious Activity Report decision is due within **30 calendar days** of initial detection.

## 900.3 Escalation to Fraud Operations

Escalate within **1 hour** of detection:

- Confirmed or suspected account takeover, including credential compromise or a contact-detail change followed by a funds request.
- Wire or ACH activity meeting a hold condition under Policy OPS-520 §520.4.
- Check kiting patterns or deposits of items exceeding **$50,000** against uncollected funds.
- Any customer report of a romance, investment, or impersonation scam in progress.

## 900.4 Escalation to Branch Management

Escalate the same business day:

- Fee waiver requests above the RM's tier limit under Policy OPS-215.
- Wire limit exception requests under Policy OPS-520.
- Aggregate customer exposure exceeding **$250,000**, or any loan **60 or more days delinquent**.
- Complaints alleging discrimination, UDAAP concerns, or any written complaint naming a regulator.

## 900.5 Escalation to Deposit Operations Supervisor

- All Regulation E dispute drafts, which cannot be filed by an RM (Policy OPS-410 §410.4).
- Adjustments or reversals of posted entries above **$1,000**.
