# Know Your Customer and Customer Identification Program

**Policy BSA-100 · BSA/AML Compliance Manual · Effective March 1, 2025**

## 100.1 Purpose

This policy implements the Customer Identification Program (CIP) required by Section 326 of the USA PATRIOT Act and 31 CFR 1020.220. No account may be opened, and no signer added, until CIP is complete.

## 100.2 Minimum Required Information

Before opening any account, the Bank must collect and record, for each customer and each authorized signer:

1. **Full legal name.**
2. **Date of birth** — the customer must be at least 18 years of age, or the account must be titled as a custodial account.
3. **Residential or business street address.** A post office box is not acceptable as the sole address; an APO/FPO address or the address of a next of kin is acceptable for customers without a street address.
4. **Taxpayer identification number** — a Social Security Number for U.S. persons, or an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number, passport number with country of issuance, or alien identification card number for non-U.S. persons.

## 100.3 Acceptable Identification Documents

At least **one unexpired government-issued photo identification** is required:

- U.S. state driver's license or state identification card
- U.S. passport or passport card
- Permanent Resident Card (Form I-551)
- U.S. military identification card
- Foreign passport bearing a valid U.S. visa or I-94 record

A **second form of identification** is required where the primary document is a foreign passport, where the customer is opening an account remotely, or where the initial deposit exceeds **$25,000**. Acceptable secondary documents include a Social Security card, a utility bill dated within 60 days, a current lease, or a bank statement dated within 60 days.

## 100.4 Verification Timeline

Documentary verification must be completed **at account opening**. Where non-documentary verification is used, it must complete within **30 calendar days** of the account opening date. Accounts unverified after 30 days are restricted from outbound activity and closed at **60 days**.

## 100.5 Enhanced Due Diligence and Recordkeeping

Enhanced Due Diligence applies to politically exposed persons, cash-intensive businesses, money services businesses, and any customer whose expected monthly activity exceeds **$100,000**. EDD files require documented source of funds and annual review. CIP records retain for **five years after the account closes**. Any name match against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list is escalated per the Escalation Matrix and the account is not opened pending compliance clearance.
