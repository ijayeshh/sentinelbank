# Consumer Deposit Fee Schedule and Waiver Authority

**Policy OPS-215 · Retail Banking Operations Manual · Revised April 1, 2025**

## 215.1 Monthly Maintenance Fees

| Product | Monthly Fee | Waiver Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Essential Checking | $12.00 | $1,500 average daily balance, or $500 in monthly direct deposits |
| Premier Checking | $25.00 | $10,000 combined deposit balance |
| Statement Savings | $5.00 | $300 minimum daily balance |
| Money Market | $15.00 | $2,500 minimum daily balance |

Maintenance fees assess on the last business day of the statement cycle. Accounts held by customers under age 24 or over age 65 are exempt.

## 215.2 Overdraft and Returned Item Fees

An **Overdraft Fee of $35.00** applies to each item paid into a negative balance. A **Returned Item Fee of $35.00** applies to each item returned unpaid. No more than **three** such fees may assess per business day, for a daily cap of $105.00. Items of **$5.00 or less** are excluded, and no fee assesses where the end-of-day negative balance is **$50.00 or less**. An **Extended Overdraft Fee of $15.00** assesses on the fifth consecutive business day an account remains negative.

## 215.3 Wire Transfer Fees

Domestic outgoing wire: **$30.00**. International outgoing wire: **$50.00**. Incoming wire (domestic or international): **$15.00**. Wire recall or trace request: **$25.00**. Premier Checking customers receive two free incoming wires per statement cycle.

## 215.4 Other Service Fees

Stop payment: $32.00. Cashier's check: $10.00. Paper statement: $3.00 per cycle. Standard card replacement: no charge. Expedited card replacement: $25.00 (see Policy OPS-330). Legal process or levy handling: $125.00.

## 215.5 Waiver Authority Limits

Fee waivers are granted by tier and are cumulative **per customer per calendar month**:

- **RM Tier 1 (Associate Relationship Manager):** up to **$50.00** per customer per month.
- **RM Tier 2 (Senior Relationship Manager):** up to **$150.00** per customer per month.
- **Branch Manager:** up to **$500.00** per customer per month.
- **Regional Operations Director:** above $500.00, and all waivers of legal process fees.

Any waiver exceeding the acting employee's tier limit must be drafted and routed for approval; it may not be applied directly. Waivers of Regulation E provisional credit fees under Policy OPS-410 do not count against these limits.
