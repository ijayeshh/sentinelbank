# Wire Transfer Limits and Callback Verification

**Policy OPS-520 · Retail Banking Operations Manual · Effective February 1, 2025**

## 520.1 Cutoff Times

Domestic wires submitted after **4:00 PM ET** and international wires submitted after **2:00 PM ET** are processed the following business day. Wires are not processed on weekends or Federal Reserve holidays.

## 520.2 Limits by Account Type

| Account Type | Per-Transaction Limit | Daily Aggregate Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Essential Checking | $10,000 | $25,000 |
| Premier Checking | $50,000 | $100,000 |
| Money Market | $50,000 | $100,000 |
| Statement Savings | $5,000 | $10,000 |
| Small Business Checking | $100,000 | $250,000 |

International wires are capped at **$50,000 per transaction** regardless of account type. Accounts opened fewer than **30 calendar days** ago are limited to **$2,500 per day** and may not send international wires. Requests above the applicable limit require a drafted exception routed to the Branch Manager; the limit may not be raised by the Relationship Manager taking the request.

## 520.3 Callback Verification Thresholds

Callback verification is an outbound telephone call placed by the Bank to a phone number **on record for at least 30 days** — never to a number supplied on the wire request itself.

- **Under $5,000:** no callback required for in-branch requests with photo ID.
- **$5,000 and above:** callback required for all channels.
- **$25,000 and above:** callback required, plus verification of two out-of-wallet questions.
- **$100,000 and above:** callback required, plus **dual authorization** by two employees, one of whom must be a Branch Manager or above.

All international wires require callback regardless of amount.

## 520.4 Mandatory Hold Conditions

A wire is held pending Fraud Operations review where any of the following applies: the beneficiary was added within the last **3 business days**; the customer's contact phone or email changed within the last **10 calendar days**; the request cites cryptocurrency, a real-estate closing, or an unexpected refund; or the customer declines the callback. Any beneficiary, originator, or intermediary bank matching the OFAC SDN list is blocked and escalated to Compliance immediately.

## 520.5 Recall

Wire recall requests must be submitted within **the same business day**. Recall is a request only; the Bank cannot guarantee return of funds once a wire has settled.
