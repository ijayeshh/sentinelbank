# Debit Card Replacement Procedure

**Policy OPS-330 · Retail Banking Operations Manual · Effective January 15, 2025**

## 330.1 Scope

This procedure covers replacement of consumer debit cards reported lost, stolen, damaged, or compromised. Card replacement does **not** itself resolve unauthorized transactions; disputed transactions are handled separately under Policy OPS-410.

## 330.2 Immediate Actions

Upon a report of a lost or stolen card, the employee taking the report must, in order:

1. **Verify the caller's identity** using two out-of-wallet questions before discussing any account detail.
2. **Place a hard block on the card immediately**, during the call. A hard block is irreversible; the card cannot be reactivated if later found.
3. Record the **date and time the customer first noticed** the loss. Regulation E liability tiers depend on this timestamp: consumer liability is capped at **$50** where the loss is reported within **2 business days** of discovery, and at **$500** where reported after 2 business days but within **60 calendar days** of the statement date.
4. Review the prior **30 days** of card activity with the customer and note any transactions the customer does not recognize.
5. Order the replacement card.

## 330.3 Delivery Standards

**Standard delivery** is 7–10 business days by first-class mail, at no charge, to the address of record. A replacement will not be mailed to an address changed within the last **30 calendar days**; in that case the card must be issued to the branch for in-person pickup with photo ID.

**Expedited delivery** is 2–3 business days by courier, at a fee of **$25.00** (see Policy OPS-215). Expedited shipping is approved without fee where any of the following criteria is met:

- The card was blocked due to **confirmed fraud** or a merchant data breach notification.
- The customer is **traveling** and will be away from the address of record for more than 7 days.
- The account has no other active card or funding method.
- The customer is age **65 or older** or enrolled in the accessibility program.

Fee waivers outside these criteria follow the tier limits in Policy OPS-215.

## 330.4 Instant Issue and PIN

Branches with instant-issue capability may print an unembossed replacement on the spot for balances under **$100,000**. PINs are never disclosed by an employee; the customer sets the PIN by phone or in the mobile app. Cards not activated within **45 days** of issue are automatically cancelled.
